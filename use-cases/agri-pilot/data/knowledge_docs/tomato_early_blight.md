# Early Blight (Alternaria solani) - Tomato

Early blight is a fungal disease that first appears on older, lower leaves as
small brown spots with a target-like ring pattern. Left untreated it spreads
upward and can defoliate the plant, reducing yield.

Recommended verified response:
- Remove and destroy infected lower leaves to slow spread.
- Improve air circulation by spacing plants and staking or caging.
- Avoid overhead irrigation; water at the base of the plant.
- Rotate crops on a 2-3 year cycle; do not follow tomato with potato.
- A copper-based or chlorothalonil fungicide labelled for early blight on
  tomato may be applied according to the product label. Follow the label
  rate exactly; do not exceed it and observe the pre-harvest interval.

# Septoria Leaf Spot (Septoria lycopersici) - Tomato

Septoria leaf spot causes many small, circular spots with dark borders and
tan/gray centers, usually starting on lower leaves during warm, humid, wet
periods. Unlike early blight the spots are smaller and lack a target-ring
pattern, and small black dots (pycnidia) are often visible in the spot
center.

Recommended verified response:
- Remove and destroy infected leaves as soon as spots appear.
- Mulch around the base of the plant to reduce soil splash onto leaves.
- Avoid overhead irrigation; water at the base of the plant, ideally in
  the morning so foliage dries quickly.
- Space and stake plants to improve airflow.
- A copper-based or chlorothalonil fungicide labelled for septoria leaf
  spot on tomato may be applied according to the product label. Follow
  the label rate exactly and observe the pre-harvest interval.
